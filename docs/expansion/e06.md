# e06 · *Thin Ice* (FINAL design, redesigned after the originality review)

**Fährhafen Lütjenbrook, a train-ferry port on the Holstein Baltic coast (fictional port, real coast) · 19 January 1945, 16:40 · A frozen ferry harbour at winter dusk · `temperate` with the frost grading and a snow dusting · 180 × 120 m · difficulty 6 of 8**

Campaign *Lights Out* (`BCD`). Conventions as e01. Guest: **Grethe Holm** (role `grethe`), playable from the start, in the grey uniform of a signals auxiliary (campaign.md §5). File `src/missions/expansion/e06_thin_ice.js`; registration: `title 'Thin Ice'`, `place 'Fährhafen Lütjenbrook, Holstein, Germany'`, `date '1945-01-19'`, `pin { lat: 54.35, lon: 11.05 }`.

## 0. Provenance and changes

The previous e06 (*Bascule*: a canal town, a bascule bridge raised by a puppet for the escape launch, a patrol stranded at the span, a Gestapo escort, a female courier's lipstick, a briefcase from an officer's room, a launch out along the canal) was flagged as the 1999 canal-town mission in profile. **Setting, layout and objectives are replaced.** Kept: the slot, the frozen dusk, the drawbridge-and-switch mechanic worked by a puppet (taught in e05, reused here in a new shape), the pushable wagon (taught in e04), Grethe and her lure, the Spy's uniform ladder, the vehicle that leaves on alarm unless its driver is dealt with (e02's lesson), the civilian constraint, the sea mines (now a tool and a hazard, not an exit).

- **A train-ferry port, not a canal town.** The last of *Referat N*'s station equipment (receivers and the key-table press) sits in a sealed Reichsbahn wagon on the **ferry berth**, waiting for a ferry that cannot come until the ice-breaker clears the channel. The berth's **link-span** is the catalogue's drawbridge: **raised** when no ferry is in, **lowered** by the span master's switch. The objective is to **push the wagon over the lowered span and off its end into the harbour.**
- **No launch, no boat.** The team walks out **over the sea ice** north-east of the mole, through a mine barrage frozen into the ice.
- **No Gestapo.** The wagon's escort is a pair of **Feldgendarmerie** NCOs (lone sergeants, rank 1, KO-able). The Gestapo stay in e03, e05 and e08.
- **No documents.** The second objective is the **searchlight tower at the harbour mouth**, which would find anything on the ice.
- **Grethe is an insider, not a courier**: in her auxiliary's uniform she walks the terminal's signals office as one of the staff; her lure is **small talk**.
- Count 33/41, par 1020/1110, unchanged.

## 2. Briefing (our own words)

**Historical.** "January 1945. Ansbach has lost his Baltic station and his index, and he is moving what is left of *Referat N* out of the Reich by train ferry: a sealed wagon with the receivers and the machine that prints his key tables, bound for Denmark and a quiet pier in Norway. The wagon stands on the berth at Lütjenbrook. The ferry has not come: the channel froze three nights ago and the port's ice-breaker clears it a little each day. Tomorrow it will be through."

**Colonel.** "Four of you and Grethe, who has a uniform that gets her into any signals office in Germany. The wagon is the last of *Referat N*'s hardware; I want it at the bottom of the harbour, and I want it done without a bang: the port is a Kriegsmarine flak station and the barracks is two hundred metres from the berth. The berth's link-span is up because there is no ferry in; the span master lowers it from his cabin, and he raises it again the moment he can. Lower it, roll the wagon over the end and let the Baltic have it. A shunting engine sits behind the wagon with its driver asleep in the cab: if the port's alarm sounds, he pulls the wagon inland and you have lost. See to him first. A searchlight tower stands at the harbour mouth: put its crew out, because you leave over the sea ice north-east of the mole, through a barrage of mines frozen in since Christmas. Walk between them. The fishing families on the west quay are not part of this. Not one of them is to be hurt."

**Notebook hints.**
- The ice holds a man. It does not hold a man where the breaker has been: the dark water is the channel and the lead round the tug.
- A puppet works a switch like any soldier. The span master sees a sentry at the switch and nothing wrong. Then he raises the span again, unless you have seen to him.
- Grethe can stop a soldier with a question. A man answering a question does not see who walks behind him.
- The Feldgendarmes are sergeants: they know a private's face. Take one of their tunics before you walk among them.
- A shunting engine needs its driver. He sleeps in the cab.
- A rifle bullet sets off a mine from a long way away. Everyone on the quay runs towards the bang.
