# e01 · *Dry Stone* (as built)

**Contrada Sant'Elia, Valle d'Itria, Puglia (fictional hamlet, real valley) · 14 September 1943, late afternoon · heat
haze over trulli, olive groves and dry-stone walls · `temperate` (dry, late-summer grading) · 120 × 140 m · LIGHTS OUT
mission 1 of 8, the tutorial**

Campaign *Lights Out* (internal id `BCD`). Metres from the NW corner, +x east, +z south; headings, `look`, `sweep` in
degrees from +x towards +z (0 = E, 90 = S, 180 = W, 270 = N). This document is kept in step with what is built:
`src/missions/expansion/e01_dry_stone.js` (data), `src/art/e01-dressing.js` + `src/art/e01-kit.js` (art),
`tools/solutions/e01.solution.mjs` + `e01.walkthrough.mjs` (the reference plan), `tests/missions/e01.test.mjs` and
`tests/e01-solution.test.mjs` (acceptance). The design it was built from is
`commandos-shots/expansion/design/final/e01.md`; §0 lists every change from it.

Original mission: no layout, objective, placement, briefing or character of any published Commandos expansion is used.

## 2. Briefing (our own words)

Historical, colonel text and orders as in the design (§2 there), narrated (assets/audio/narration/e01). Notebook hints,
as built:

- A man you knock out wakes in about half a minute and shouts. Handcuff him and he stays quiet until a comrade finds him. The cuffs take his cigarettes; you will want them later.
- A stone thrown near a guard makes him look at it. Three in quick succession bring a walking guard over to search; a sentry at his post only looks.
- The field walls are low. A man lying behind one is hidden; a man standing is not.
- The Driver's rifle kills with one round at thirty paces and is heard at twenty. Count the men within twenty paces before you fire.
- If the farm's alarm sounds, Steinmetz burns his tables. Get to him first.
- The Sapper's charges are long-fused today, four minutes: set the hut's when the farm is all but yours, and be gone when it blows.
- A cuffed man fits through a doorway: carry him into a trullo, the sentry box or a field hut and nobody will find him.
- In this heat haze a sentry's eyes carry less far than usual. Use it.
- Carry a handcuffed prisoner to the lorry and put him in the back. Nobody stops a German lorry on a German road. They stop men on foot.

## 3. Map (as built)

- **South-west: the olive grove** (start (12–16, 124–128)), walled (field walls with gates north and east).
- **The hamlet** (x 36–75, z 92–132): eight trulli round a piazza with a well, the chapel (44, 93.6) on the road in from
  the west, the **jazzo** (a walled sheepfold, x 63–72, z 101–112) between the piazza and the checkpoint, its south wall
  breached onto the piazza. Two foragers; no zone.
- **The road** dog-legs round the jazzo to the **checkpoint**: boom (72.6, 98.6, rot 19°), sentry box (77, 99.6,
  enterable), a wayside shrine; then north-east past the field wall `wl_cp` to the farm's gate (88, 76); from the gate
  east along the wall and north past the **cistern tower** (104, 24) to the **exit** (114.2, 4, r 6).
- **The masseria** (x 76–110, z 40–76): rubble walls 3 m, one arched gate in the south wall (buttress + crate stack
  inside), the house along the north wall, the **tower** at its east end (sentry g2 on top), the **radio room** below it
  (W doorway; the map table with the key-table case, the set, the stove), the **officers' wing** on the east side
  (enterable), the **stables** on the west side (the garrison; drv1 asleep inside), the DF lorry and the detachment's
  **lorry** (97, 66.4) in the yard.
- **West of the farm: the threshing floor** in its wire fence, two lattice masts and the **coupling hut** (58, 40.8;
  `destroy` o3, bomb only).
- **The casedda** (78.4, 80.6): a field trullo, enterable, outside the compound's SW corner.
- **Fields** north and east, divided by dry-stone walls (`low`: crawl cover), almonds, figs, hay stooks; the **knoll**
  with a ruined trullo (30, 68).
- **The dry-stone walls** (`drystone_field`, `src/art/e01-kit.js`; the field walls and the jazzo): a battered core laid
  dry (stacked flat stones, dark voids, no mortar), split-limestone face stones standing proud in staggered courses, flat
  cap slabs laid across at odd angles (the cap's top within 8 cm of the gameplay height), tumbled free ends and a few
  stones fallen at the foot. Everything inside the gameplay line and its ends but the fallen stones (ankle-high, within
  a hand's breadth); `tests/unit/e01-kit.test.mjs`. The look is drawn only (`userData.clip = false`): what gameplay
  measures of a wall — the visual nav stamp's bands, sight, tops — is the walls' first geometry, built from the same
  seed and kept undrawn under it (`art/ruin-kit.js` `navProxy`, as the ruined walls of the base missions). Measured, the
  new look's proud stones crossed 0.25 m cell lines: 256 more blocked cells over the walls, 32 along the jazzo's north
  wall, and the Easy route past its corner had no way through (the GPU solution test caught it; the headless runs play
  the nav cache, which had the first look's cells). The unit test pins the measured cells of the jazzo and of the
  checkpoint's field wall to the first look's. The undrawn meshes are kept out of the GTAO normal prepass too
  (`userData.aoExclude`: that pass swaps every visible mesh's material for its own and drew them — 15 draws, 80 k
  triangles, and ambient occlusion of a wall nobody sees); the render budget is back to 315 draws, 3.21 M triangles.

## 6. Set pieces and mechanics

- **Key tables** (`evidence`): on the map table; Use takes them (o2). On RMAS, a free Steinmetz walks to the stove and
  burns them (Hard 25 s, Easy 40 s): the arc closed, the mission fails ("THE KEY TABLES ARE ASH.").
- **Stow** cuffed men in the chapel, trullo t1, the sentry box, the casedda (any enterable building).
- **Captive cargo**: Steinmetz carried to the lorry is put in the back.
- **The lorry**: the Driver at the wheel; an untainted German lorry passes everyone; a tainted one is fired on.
- **Halt sweeps**, **heat haze**, **practice area**, **bump taint**: docs/expansion/MECHANICS.md.
