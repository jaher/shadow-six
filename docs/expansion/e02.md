## 0. Provenance and changes

Base: the variety-first *Curfew* (theatres/e02.md), kept almost whole: it is the campaign's first night, its first guest and the Spy's wardrobe ladder, and it is the mission that introduces Tonino as the shell defines him. Changes by the judge:
- **Three outs for the van** (grafted from story/e02.md's lorry clock): the DF van has a named **driver** asleep in its cab. The van leaves on the palazzo alarm only if he is free; bind him (or take the log) and the fail rule is disarmed. The player learns that a vehicle needs a driver.
- **Searchlight** simplified to what the engine has: the roof crew is an `elevated` sentry with a long narrow vision profile (request `vision: 'searchlight'`; fallback the `mg` profile). `o2` is his death or capture, the taught route being the Sniper from the belfry. The generator lever darkens the gate floodlights (cosmetic) and is not an objective.
- **Hollis** is a rescued `guest` with `follow: 'team'` (MECHANICS.md escort).
- Ambient **insurgents** (`pow`) behind the barricade and a wounded man in a doorway: the quarter is alive; killing a civilian is not possible for the enemy here (no alarm reaches the barricade strip) but the team cannot shoot through them.
- Count 21/30 (the driver added).

## 2. Briefing (our own words)

**Historical.** "Naples, the last days of September 1943. The city has risen against the garrison before our army arrives. The Germans are rounding up men for labour in the north and blowing the port behind them. One thing worries London more than the port: a direction-finding team of *Referat N* has moved into a palazzo in the Quartiere Scirocco and is taking bearings on the insurgents' transmitter. With it works our liaison officer, Captain Hollis. Last night they took him."

**Colonel.** "You go in with a guide, a boy called Tonino. His father was killed in the summer raids; he knows the quarter stair by stair and the soldiers buy their cigarettes from him. Trust him. Three jobs. One: the DF team keeps its bearings in a log in the van parked in the palazzo courtyard; bring it out and the transmitter stays hidden. Two: a searchlight on the palazzo roof sweeps the avenue every night; put it out and the quarter can move again. Three: Hollis is in the palazzo cellar. Get him and everyone else into the old aqueduct cistern on the upper street before dawn. The Spy will need better clothes than he has: start with the washing lines."

**Notebook hints.**
- Tonino cannot climb, but he can put a stone through a window at twenty paces and a packet of cigarettes at a soldier's feet.
- A soldier who sees a packet land near him walks over, kneels and lights up. For three seconds he sees nothing but the match.
- A private's tunic fools privates. Sergeants know their own men; officers know everyone. Take a better uniform off a better man: the hanger works on anyone you have knocked out.
- The dog patrol in the cross-alley does not care what the Spy is wearing.
- If the palazzo sounds its alarm the van leaves with the log. A van needs a driver; he sleeps in the cab.
