# e04 · *Black Rain* (FINAL design, redesigned after the originality review)

**Puits Sainte-Barbe colliery, Creutzberg, Moselle, Lorraine (fictional pit, real coal basin) · 11 August 1944, 14:20 · Summer rain over a pithead, its spoil heap and its sidings · `temperate` (overcast, wet) · 200 × 110 m · difficulty 4 of 8**

Campaign *Lights Out* (`BCD`). Conventions as e01. No guest. File `src/missions/expansion/e04_black_rain.js`; registration: `title 'Black Rain'`, `place 'Puits Sainte-Barbe, Creutzberg, Lorraine, France'`, `date '1944-08-11'`, `pin { lat: 49.15, lon: 6.75 }`.

## 0. Provenance and changes

The previous e04 (*Dead Slow*: a marshalling yard with a half-roundhouse, a listening train as the objective, a box wagon pushed 120 m along a siding as moving cover, a wagon rolled onto the exit points) was flagged as the 1999 railway-yard mission in profile. **Setting, layout and objectives are replaced.** Kept: the slot, the rain and the mud, the pushable wagon as moving cover (taught here, reused in e06), the puppet on a machine one mission after it is taught, the Lee-Enfield's doctrine, the lieutenant, the through-train as a hazard and a screen, and the arc beat (the archive of bearings that would have found a hundred sets).

- **A colliery, not a yard.** *Referat N*'s direction-finding post sits on the **spoil heap** (the highest ground for thirty kilometres) with its intercept room in the pithead's lamp room. The rails are the pit's own: a **tub tramway** across the yard and a **cable incline** up the heap.
- **The puppet works a winch.** The incline carriage is winch-only and the winding house's window faces the heap sentry: a cuffed winchman on strings sends three commandos up the heap inside a spoil skip while the Green Beret climbs the gully.
- **The lift's first use** is the incline (the catalogue's lift and winch), reused in e05 (the fort's hoist).
- **No train objective, no points, no roundhouse.** The quiet-or-lose rule is **the shaft**: on alarm the station chief drops the bearing archive down the pit.
- **Out over the heap**, down its far side, with the DF lorries blown behind by remote from the top.
- Count 28/35, par 840/920 in the design; the build's numbers are in **As built** below.

## 2. Briefing (our own words)

**Historical.** "August 1944. Our armies are breaking out of Normandy and the Germans are pulling their special units east. One of them is *Referat N*'s direction-finding post for eastern France: since the winter it has worked from the top of a colliery spoil heap in the Lorraine coal basin, where its loop antenna sees every transmitter between Nancy and the Saar. Its bearing archive has found a hundred sets. The post is packing up to go. It leaves tomorrow."

**Colonel.** "Four of you, in daylight, in the rain. The archive is in the station chief's office in the pithead lamp room; bring it out before anyone can throw it down the shaft, which is what they are told to do. Then the heap: the DF hut on top goes up with a charge, and their two lorries in the yard go up by remote when you are clear. The heap's only road is a cable incline worked from the winding house at its foot, and the winchman never leaves his drum. Handcuff him and work him like a puppet: a winchman sends up a skip and nobody looks twice. The Green Beret can climb the gully on the heap's south side; nobody else can. You leave over the top and down the far side into the woods. Mind the main line along the north fence: a coal train comes through every two minutes and does not stop. And mind the slurry: it holds your footprints, and the yard walkers follow them."

**Notebook hints.**
- A spoil wagon pushed along the tramway is a wall that walks with you. Keep it between you and the eyes that matter.
- The Driver's rifle kills at thirty paces with one round, but it is as loud as a pistol. Shoot where nobody is within twenty paces of you.
- The coal train hides whatever is on the far side of it for ten seconds. It also kills whatever is on its track.
- The incline carriage moves only when the winch is worked. A handcuffed winchman on strings works it like any winchman. The sentry on the heap sees a railwayman at his drum and nothing wrong.
- If the alarm sounds in the pithead before you have the archive, the chief carries it to the shaft.
