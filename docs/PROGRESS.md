- 2026-09-27: PHASE 3 step 1/3 WIND (feat/phase3, docs/wind-pipeline.md).
  - Wind model: `world/wind.js` WindField, one per mission (`world.wind`). Wind comes from theater presets, a calm
    preset at dawn and dusk, or the mission's optional `weather.wind` field. It combines gust fronts travelling
    downwind, turbulence, lulls and desert dust devils. It is a pure function of sim time, so it is deterministic and
    freezes on pause, and the CPU `sample()` and GLSL `windSample()` twins share one uniform set.
  - Consumers:
    - Grass: gust waves and sheen.
    - Trees: hierarchical wind (trunk bend at the natural frequency, branch sway, leaf/needle flutter, palm-frond
