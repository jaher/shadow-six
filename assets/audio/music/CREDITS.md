# Music credits

All music here is **original work composed for SHADOW SIX**: symbolic scores (route A) rendered offline with the
VSCO 2 Community Edition and VSCO 1 percussion sample libraries (Versilian Studios — Sam Gossner, Simon Dalzell;
sample cutting by Elan Hickler/Soundemote; CC0 1.0, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE). No library music, no
AI-generated audio, no third-party recordings. Nothing quotes or paraphrases the Commandos scores or any film score
(STYLE §4.4 originality screen: 5-note interval windows, transposition-invariant, 17 reference incipits; the fanfare
motif M4 was rewritten in STYLE rev 2 after it matched a famous film head). A human listen against the Commandos
albums is still recommended. Licence of the music: same as the project.

Formats: `<cue>.ogg` (Vorbis q5, 48 kHz) and `<cue>.mp3` (128 kb/s, Safari). WAV masters stay outside the repo.
Loudness: front end −18 LUFS, briefings −20, stingers −16, mission score −22 (alert −19); true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
`manifest.json` lists every cue's files, loop points (seconds and samples @ 48 kHz), category, gain, key and tempo.
